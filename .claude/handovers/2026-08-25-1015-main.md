## 9. Constraints learned this session

- **[VERIFIED — four live pages at 1440]** `templates/collection.json` sets
  `allow_transparent_header: true`; on a photographic banner the nav becomes unreadable. Off
  individually on creams-moisturizers, serums, fine-lines-wrinkles, brightening-glow.
- **[VERIFIED — hit twice]** `publish-collection-banner-template.py` **rebuilds the page
  template from the shared default on every run**, silently discarding that fix and any other
  tuning. Re-apply after every republish. A template built by another route (e.g.
  `/collections/fine-lines-wrinkles`) must be updated with a targeted `patch-template.py`
  image_assignments patch instead.
- **[VERIFIED — both files confirmed on Shopify]** `upload-theme-images.py` could bind BOTH
  slots to one file while polling. Fixed. **Never let one uploaded stem be a prefix of
  another.** It also **requires an `alt` field** and dies with a bare `KeyError: 'alt'`.
- **[VERIFIED — measured]** Auto-detected product bounding boxes cannot tell a bottle from
  its reflection, nor find a white bulb on a white sweep. Five serum tiles carry hard-coded
  `y_override` values read off a labelled grid.
- **[VERIFIED — measured off the live CDN]** The brightening-glow banner already had
  `fetchpriority="high"`, `sizes="100vw"` and a 19-step srcset; delivery was 41.8KB at
  width=1280. Do **not** re-compress sources — rule 5 records it made this store *slower*.

## 10. Failed approaches (don't retry)

- **Lengthening the deterministic arm fade** to 600–1300px to fix a "cut off" forearm — it
  removes the hard edge but reads as a **smear**, because a 1-D edge profile dragged
  sideways has by construction no variation along its length.
- **Telling Seedream where to put the elbow as "roughly one third in from the left edge"** —
  that is an instruction to fill the left; it re-rendered zoomed in, 0px of heading ground.
  Tightening the bound from 40% to 50% then produced **no improvement at all** (34–38%);
  Seedream has a compositional floor. Do not spend a fourth wave on it.
- **Filling a grown canvas with a median background colour** — leaves a visible rectangle
  where it meets the original, because these sweeps are gradients. Replicate the edge.
- **Trusting an overlay drawn at thumbnail size to validate a bounding box** — it rejected
  two obvious errors and passed two that shipped. Judge on the rendered page, at size.
