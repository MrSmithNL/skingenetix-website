## 9. Constraints learned this session

- **`enable_parallax` crops images** — the theme's own schema says so. No supplied aspect
  ratio survives it. Read the section's settings before reworking any asset.
- **Seedream caps at 4096** but honours arbitrary ratios; **gpt-image hard-refuses beyond
  3:1**; **NBP ignores pixel dimensions** and snaps to its nearest named ratio, silently.
- **Seedream and gpt-image refuse bare-skin briefs** on content policy. Establishing clothing
  cut refusals from 13/36 to 1/48 — but naming a garment changes the picture.
- **Never `rm -rf`** — it is on the deny list and a call was refused this session for it.
- The MCP Playwright browser is usually locked by another session; use headless Chrome with
  an isolated `--user-data-dir`.
