## 9. Constraints learned this session

- **Gemini daily cap is 250 requests, shared** by NBP Pro and Flash → **two products/day**.
- **Luma has no 4:5** — Meta feed plates must route to Seedream or NBP.
- **Luma `image_edit` ignores `aspect_ratio`** and takes a single base64 `source`.
- **`square_hd` means 3072 on Seedream and 1024 on FLUX.2** — never trust a size preset
  across providers.
- **google-genai 1.47 cannot pass `imageSize`** — the Gemini adapter uses raw REST.
- **FLUX.2 invents brands** — barred from anything where branding is legible.
- **`assets/` is gitignored** (9.7 GB) — the reference crops live there and are NOT backed
  up by the repo; they regenerate from Drive via the script.
- **The generated Matrixyl carton cannot be regenerated** — it is source material in Drive.
- `.husky` hooks are not executable, so git hooks do not run. Gates must be run by hand.

## 10. Failed approaches (don't retry)

- **Automatic product/carton segmentation on the pack shots.** Seven attempts. Five failed
  for one reason: white subject on a white sweep. Strict thresholds drop the white pipette
  bulb; loose ones latch onto the background gradient; component masking bites chunks out
  of silver cartons; and in most shots the two objects _touch_, so there is no gap to find.
  **Use the measured per-image boundaries in the script.**
- **Expanding a crop outward to reach square.** A tall narrow bottle yields a square as
  wide as it is tall and drags in the neighbour. Pad to square instead.
- **Estimating a boundary.** 0.45 left a bottle sliver, 0.50 sliced the carton; measured it
  is 0.488. Measure from the column-occupancy profile.
- **`gemini-2-flash-image`** — does not exist, 404s silently and costs a whole backend.
- **`input_fidelity` on gpt-image-2** — rejected with a 400 (it worked on gpt-image-1).
