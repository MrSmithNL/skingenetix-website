## 10. Failed approaches (don't retry)

- **Saturation-threshold detection of "is the block inset"** — on r3's warm peach ground it swallowed the
  background and reported ~7/30 usable when the truth was **30/30**. Use vertical-gradient structure
  instead, and **draw the detected bounds on the image and look** before acting.
- **Three separate automated bbox attempts on a Luma frame** (saturation, deviation-from-fitted-ground,
  per-row edge reference) all returned the full frame — its vignette defeats any global threshold. A
  labelled pixel grid read by eye took one pass and was right.
- **Tone-matching a transplant against the target's own low frequency** when the target contains the text
  being removed — it re-darkens exactly where the letters were, leaving a ghost. Use normalised
  convolution with the text pixels excluded.
- **Reframing a full-bleed block** — a block clipped by the frame has no end faces, so narrowing it
  leaves flat 2D slices. Malcolm rejected two attempts before the cause was understood.
