## Clinical research

Field `custom.clinical_research`:

PDRN, the active in this cream, has been compared with retinol in a randomised, double-blind, split-face
clinical study.

- Design: 31 women aged 35 to 55 with sensitive skin. A PDRN eye cream (0.1% PDRN) went on one side of the face
  and a
  retinol eye cream (0.1% retinol) on the other, twice daily for 28 days.
- Crow's feet: wrinkle area and number fell more on the PDRN side than on the retinol side at day 28.
- Timing: the smoothing on the PDRN side was already evident at day 14.
- Firmness and elasticity: instrument readings improved more on the PDRN side.
- Tolerability: no adverse reactions were reported on either side.

Ye et al. 2026, PLOS ONE. There was no placebo arm, so retinol was the only comparator. The study tested PDRN
alone in an
eye cream; it did not test collagen. Four of the authors list affiliations with commercial biotechnology
companies; the
paper declares no conflicts and no specific funding. Results describe the ingredient as studied, not this
product; the
full figures are on the [PDRN research page](/pages/pdrn-research).

## FAQ

Seven items: six on the People Also Ask questions in the plan (Part 3.2), plus one safety item (the last).

### What not to mix with PDRN cream?

No study has tested PDRN together with retinol, vitamin C or exfoliating acids in one routine, so there is no
research-based
"do not mix" list. In the one trial that set PDRN against retinol, the two were on opposite sides of the face,
not layered.
Add one new active at a time, patch test, and stop using a product if stinging or redness does not settle.

Skin freshly opened by a procedure is a different case. In a trial of a polynucleotide serum applied straight after
clinic microneedling, the product was a different one, used on treated skin
([our appraisal](/blogs/clinical-studies/pdrn-microneedling-split-face-trial-yogya-2022)). This cream is for
intact skin.

### Is PDRN salmon sperm?

PDRN is extracted from the sperm cells of salmon and purified to remove proteins, leaving DNA fragments. On an
ingredient
list it appears as Sodium DNA. This cream's PDRN is salmon-derived, and its collagen is of animal origin, so the
cream is not
vegan. Products sold as "vegan PDRN" use polynucleotides from plants or other non-animal sources, which are a
different
ingredient.

### How do you use a PDRN cream, and when should you put it on?

Put it on in the evening as the last step. After cleansing and your serum, press a pea-sized to two-pea-sized
amount over
the face and neck. Use it nightly. In the study, PDRN was applied twice a day for 28 days, so give any PDRN
routine at
least 4 weeks before judging it, and use SPF in the morning.

### Does PDRN cream work?

The one controlled human study of PDRN on intact skin compared a PDRN eye cream with a retinol eye cream in 31
women over
28 days. The PDRN side improved more on crow's feet, firmness and elasticity, and the smoothing was evident by
day 14.
That study used 0.1% PDRN and did not test our cream, so it describes the ingredient. Read
[our appraisal](/blogs/clinical-studies/pdrn-vs-retinol-split-face-trial-ye-2026) or the
[PDRN research page](/pages/pdrn-research).

### Can I use it with the PDRN Renewal Serum?

Yes, they are designed to layer. Apply the [PDRN 1% Renewal Serum](/products/pdrn-renewal-serum) first, let it
absorb for
about a minute, then seal it in with the night cream.

### What does 1% PDRN mean?

1% is 10,000 parts per million (ppm), and the percentage is printed on the product so you can compare like with
like. The
clinical study used 0.1% PDRN in an eye cream. No study has compared strengths, so we do not claim that 1% works
better
than 0.1%.

### Who should not use this cream?

PDRN is salmon-derived. Anyone who is allergic to fish should not use it. Patch test on a small area before
first use, and
stop using it if stinging or redness does not settle. The trial excluded people with a known PDRN allergy and
did not test
pregnancy, so ask your doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Do not apply it to broken skin.
