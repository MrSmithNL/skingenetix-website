## Clinical research

Field `custom.clinical_research`:

PDRN, the active in this serum, has been compared with retinol in a randomised, double-blind, split-face
clinical study.

- Design: 31 women aged 35 to 55 with sensitive skin. A PDRN eye cream (0.1% PDRN) went on one side of the face
  and a
  retinol eye cream (0.1% retinol) on the other, twice daily for 28 days.
- Crow's feet: wrinkle area and number fell more on the PDRN side than on the retinol side at day 28.
- Timing: the smoothing on the PDRN side was already evident at day 14.
- Firmness and elasticity: instrument readings improved more on the PDRN side.
- Tolerability: no adverse reactions were reported on either side.

Ye et al. 2026, PLOS ONE. There was no placebo arm, so retinol was the only comparator. Four of the authors list
affiliations with commercial biotechnology companies; the paper declares no conflicts and no specific funding.
Results describe the ingredient as studied, not this product; the full figures are on the
[PDRN research page](/pages/pdrn-research).

## FAQ

Seven items: six on the questions people ask, plus one safety item (the last).

### What does a PDRN serum do?

PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide, listed on the label as Sodium DNA) is a salmon-derived ingredient used in
skincare for
smoother-looking, fresher-looking skin. This serum has 1% PDRN, which is 10,000 ppm, plus copper peptide,
niacinamide
and hyaluronic acid. In a 2026 split-face clinical study, a PDRN eye cream improved crow's-feet wrinkles more than a
retinol eye cream over 28 days.

### Does PDRN serum actually work?

The one controlled human study of PDRN applied to intact skin compared a PDRN eye cream with a retinol eye cream
in 31
women over 28 days. The PDRN side improved more on crow's feet, firmness and elasticity, and the smoothing was
evident by
day 14. That study used 0.1% PDRN and did not test our serum, so it describes the ingredient. Read
[our appraisal of the trial](/blogs/clinical-studies/pdrn-vs-retinol-split-face-trial-ye-2026) or the
[PDRN research page](/pages/pdrn-research).

### How do I use a PDRN serum?

Apply it morning and evening after cleansing. Press 3-4 drops onto slightly damp skin across the face and neck, wait
about a minute, then follow with your moisturiser. Finish with SPF in the morning. The trial ran for 28 days of
twice-daily use, so give it at least 4 weeks.

### Can I use PDRN serum with retinol, and what should I not mix it with?

No study has tested PDRN together with retinol, vitamin C or exfoliating acids in one routine, so there is no
research-based
"do not mix" list. In the trial, PDRN and retinol were on opposite sides of the face, not layered. Add one new
active at
a time, patch test, and stop using a product if stinging or redness does not settle.

### Is PDRN salmon sperm? Where does it come from?

PDRN is extracted from the sperm cells of salmon and purified to remove proteins, leaving DNA fragments. On an
ingredient
list it appears as Sodium DNA. Because it is animal-derived, this serum is not vegan. Products sold as "vegan
PDRN" use
polynucleotides from plants or other non-animal sources, which are a different ingredient.

### Can I layer it with the PDRN Collagen Night Cream?

Yes. Apply the serum first, let it absorb for about a minute, then seal it with the
[PDRN 1% Collagen Night Cream](/products/pdrn-collagen-night-cream) in the evening. Use SPF in the morning.

### Who should not use this serum?

PDRN is salmon-derived. Anyone who is allergic to fish should not use it. Patch test on a small area before
first use, and
stop using it if stinging or redness does not settle. The trial excluded people with a known PDRN allergy and
did not test
pregnancy, so ask your doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Do not apply it to broken skin.
