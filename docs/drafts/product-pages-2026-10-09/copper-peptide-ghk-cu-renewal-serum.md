## Key benefits

- Firmer-looking, smoother-looking skin: 2% GHK-Cu, the copper peptide studied in a double-blind wrinkle trial
- For the look of fine lines: in that trial a GHK-Cu serum reduced crow's-feet wrinkle volume and depth
- Vitamin C and niacinamide in the same bottle: 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid, a stable form of vitamin C, and
  niacinamide sit alongside the peptide, so there is no extra step
- A hydrated, plump feel from multi-weight hyaluronic acid

What to expect: a hydrated feel from the first use. In the double-blind trial, change in the wrinkles was
measured after 4 and 8 weeks of twice-daily use of a GHK-Cu serum, so give it at least 8 weeks of regular use.

## How to use

Use morning and evening. After cleansing, press 3-4 drops onto slightly damp skin across the face and neck.
Pat gently, don't rub. Let it absorb for about a minute, then follow with your cream. In the morning, always
finish with SPF.

What to layer it with: it sits well under other peptides, hyaluronic acid and moisturisers. If you separately
use pure L-ascorbic acid vitamin C, strong acids (AHA or BHA) or a high-strength retinol, apply those at a
different time of day.

Complete the routine: the Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu) 2% Day Gel-Cream in the morning, or the Copper Peptide
(GHK-Cu) 2% Night Cream in the evening.

The three how-to steps stay as they are (1. Cleanse; 2. Apply Copper Peptide Serum, 3-4 drops, 60 seconds;
3. Moisturise + SPF 30+ every morning).

## Clinical research

**GHK-Cu, the copper peptide in this serum, has a double-blind trial behind it.**

In a randomised, double-blind, split-face trial of a GHK-Cu serum:

- 40 women aged 40 to 65 used it twice a day for 8 weeks (39 were analysed)
- Wrinkle volume and depth fell further with the GHK-Cu serum than with the plain serum base, measured by 3D
  skin imaging of a crow's-feet wrinkle
- Change was measured at week 4 and had grown by week 8
- It was well tolerated by 39 of the 40 women; one had a minor reaction, stopped, and it settled without
  treatment

Also on record:

- In a one-month skin-biopsy study of 20 volunteers, more people using a copper-binding peptide cream showed an
  increase in procollagen (the precursor of collagen) than in the other three creams tested (10 people per
  cream, thigh skin, no statistical test between the creams)
- An independent 2026 systematic review of 20 studies, two of them randomised trials, concluded that GHK-Cu
  significantly reduced wrinkle volume and depth compared with controls. It also noted a limited number of
  well-designed clinical trials

Sources: Badenhorst et al. 2016, J Aging Sci 4(3):166 (the GHK-Cu serum used a lipid nano-carrier, and the
plain serum base carried neither the peptide nor the carrier, so the difference covers both; funded by the
test serum's maker, with independent statistics); Abdulghani et al. 1998, Disease Management and Clinical
Outcomes; Mokhtar et al. 2026, Aesthetic Surgery Journal. Results describe the ingredient as studied, not
this product; the full figures are on the research page.

## FAQ

### What does GHK-Cu do for skin?

GHK-Cu is a copper peptide your own body makes, first isolated from human plasma in 1973. In a double-blind
trial, a GHK-Cu serum reduced crow's-feet wrinkle volume and depth further than the plain serum base after
8 weeks, and an independent 2026 review reached the same conclusion on wrinkle volume and depth. The human
evidence is still a handful of trials. In your routine, this serum is for firmer-looking, smoother-looking
skin.

### What is in the Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu) 2% Renewal Serum?

2% GHK-Cu, a multi-peptide complex, 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid (a stable form of vitamin C), niacinamide and
hyaluronic acid. It is a daily serum for firmer-looking, smoother-looking skin.

### What should you not mix copper peptides with?

No combination has been tested in a trial. This serum already pairs 2% GHK-Cu with a stable vitamin C and
niacinamide in one formula. If you separately use pure L-ascorbic acid vitamin C, strong acids (AHA or BHA)
or a high-strength retinol, apply those at a different time of day. Copper peptides are not acids or
exfoliants, so they layer easily with other peptides, hyaluronic acid and moisturisers.

### How often should you use copper peptides on your face?

Use this serum morning and evening: 3-4 drops on clean skin, about 60 seconds to absorb, then your cream (and
SPF in the morning). The trial used a different GHK-Cu serum twice a day for 8 weeks, and measured change at
weeks 4 and 8, so judge any copper peptide over at least 8 weeks of regular use.

### Can I layer it with the Day Gel-Cream or the Night Cream?

Yes. Apply the serum first, then seal with the Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu) 2% Day Gel-Cream in the morning or the
Night Cream in the evening.

### What are the downsides of copper peptides, and should I patch test?

In the 8-week controlled trial, 39 of 40 women tolerated a GHK-Cu serum well, with none of the peeling,
dryness or redness seen with retinoids. One woman had a minor reaction on both sides of her face, stopped,
and it settled without treatment. That trial did not state its concentration, and our 2% products have no
tolerance study of their own, so patch test first: apply a small amount behind the ear for 24 to 48 hours.
Stop using it if stinging or redness does not settle, and see a pharmacist or dermatologist if a reaction
persists. Keep it away from the eyes and broken skin. It has not been tested in pregnancy or while
breastfeeding, so ask your doctor or pharmacist first.
