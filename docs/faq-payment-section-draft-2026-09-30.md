## Proposed questions and answers

Every fact is taken from the store itself (checked 2026-09-30), not assumed:

- the payment methods are the icons Shopify prints in the footer from the store's enabled methods;
- the base currency is the euro, with local currencies enabled;
- checkout is Shopify's.

The answers make no promises about times or outcomes.

**Which payment methods do you accept?**

The main cards and wallets: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro and Union Pay; Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay
and PayPal. Depending on your country you can also use local methods: iDEAL and Wero, Bancontact, MB WAY or MobilePay.
Checkout shows only the methods available where you are.

**Which currency will I pay in?**

Our prices are set in euros, and where we can, we show them in your local currency. The total on the last checkout
page is what you pay. If your card is in another currency, your bank may add its own exchange fee.

**Is it safe to pay on your site?**

Payment runs through Shopify's checkout, and your card details go straight to the payment provider. Skingenetix never
sees or stores your full card number.

**My payment didn't go through. What should I do?**

- Check the card details and billing address, or try another payment method.
- If you were charged but got no order confirmation email, don't pay again.
- Write to us through the contact page with the email address you used and roughly when you ordered, and we'll look
  it up.
