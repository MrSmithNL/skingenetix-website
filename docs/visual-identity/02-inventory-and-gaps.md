## 4. Three things outside the imagery brief that need a decision

These surfaced during the audit. None is an art-direction problem, and none should be fixed by
generating a picture.

1. **A Hairgenetix advert plays on the Skingenetix product template.**
   `templates/product.json` → section `video_reviews` references
   `shopify://files/videos/Hairgenetix-Copper-Peptide-Hair-Growth-Serum-Ad-Horizontal (3).mp4`,
   three times. That is the *fallback* product template — any product without a bespoke template
   shows a hair-growth advert on a skincare page. **Recommend: remove the section.**

2. **Before/after images.** Two files carry the visual "results" claim across 10 and 20 slots.
   If these are AI-generated rather than photographs of real users, they are a regulatory and
   trust exposure, not a design choice. **Recommend: confirm provenance before the store takes
   orders.** Not in scope for generation.

3. **Press logos.** `product.json` shows Vogue, Forbes, Elle, Harper's Bazaar and Cosmopolitan
   logos under "featured in". Unless Skingenetix has genuinely been featured, these should come
   down. **Recommend: confirm, or remove the section.**
