# Design critique: two study pages on the stock template (2026-09-26)

**Pages judged (both live):**

1. `/pages/study/argireline-forehead-roughness-trial-raikou-2017`, built today ("Raikou")
2. `/pages/study/copper-peptide-wrinkle-trial-badenhorst-2016`, the reference build ("Badenhorst"). Its stock-section rebuild had never been critiqued.

**Critic:** `design-critic` agent, fresh context, judging the rendered pages only. **Cycle:** 1 for the stock template. The 2026-09-24 critique (REBUILD, 5.48) judged the deleted custom-HTML build, so these scores are not a before/after pair with it.

**What this was judged against.** There is no signed direction contract: no `website/design/<slug>/01-direction-contract.md`, no
`DESIGN.md`, no `02-reference-rules.md`. The pages were judged against `docs/study-page-template.md` (structure and hard rules),
`docs/science-page-template.md` §3.6 (one accent per ingredient) and `docs/visual-identity/03-art-direction-and-briefs.md` ("Clinical
Luminism", including the rule that type sits in a quiet 40% of the frame, never over the subject). **Neither template document names a
visual signature for the study page.** That gap limits these pages more than any single fault below.

**Owner rules respected, and not argued against:** stock sections before custom code; section CSS under 500 characters with no `content:`;
no black backgrounds; three identical media rows; "How to read this result" written as plain facts, positive side first; one accent per
ingredient; Raikou's desktop and phone banners differ on purpose.

## How it was captured and measured

- Playwright Chromium with a full Chrome user agent. Blocked: klaviyo, forms.shopifyapps.com, shopify-forms, gtm.js, google-analytics, connect.facebook. Cookie banner hidden. Pages fetched one at a time, with no 429s.
- Widths: 1440×900, 390×844 (at 2× pixel density) and 195×422 (390 at 200% zoom). Menu opened at 390, links hit-tested, then closed with Escape.
- `measure.py <url> 1440` and `… 390` on both pages. Two of its numbers are artefacts on this theme and were checked a second way:
  - `distinctSectionPaddings = 1` happens because every Impact section reports `padding-top: 0`: the theme pads an inner wrapper instead. A second probe measured the real gap from each section's top edge to its first content.
  - `measureCh` is real on these pages (102 at 1440, 49 at 390). The probe traced it to the answer paragraphs, not to the cookie dialog.
  - `measure.py` does not block Klaviyo, so its script weight and tap-target counts include the pop-up and the cookie buttons.
- Hero contrast method: hide the hero text, screenshot the banner, take the 98th-percentile background brightness under each text line and compare it with white text. Also reported: the worst 80px slice of each line.
- Evidence sheet: `~/Desktop/skingenetix-study-critique-renders.png`. Raw captures and JSON are in the session scratchpad (`…/scratchpad/crit/`); they were not copied into the repo.

## Theme-wide items (not charged to the template; listed so nobody fixes them here)

- The announcement-bar marquee loops every 11.6s forever with no pause control (WCAG 2.2.2). Human check needed: it may stop on hover or under reduced motion.
- At 195px, the footer newsletter form (min width 200px) causes the 25px overflow, and the header logo overlaps the icons.
- Page weight is 2.1–2.2 MB, including 836 KB of script.
- Tap targets: 67 of 93 are under 44px at 1440 and 55 of 61 at 390, almost all in the nav, footer and inline links.
- The mobile menu **passes**: it opens as a drawer with large targets and closes on Escape.

## Answers

1. **Signature.**
   - **Raikou:** the one idea is its desktop banner, "a wave of light losing amplitude until it lies flat" (a relaxed expression line). But the wave runs under the text, and the phone swaps it out. The page itself has **no signature**.
   - **Badenhorst:** **none**.
   - **Template:** its content idea, one trial appraised from the full text with every figure dated and sourced, is real. The design does not express it: nothing visually separates the appraisal from the method summary.
2. **Three tells that this was generated rather than designed:**
   - nine section titles at one size (48px, weight 300, centred), separated by one 80px gap;
   - seven rounded white boxes on bone, one with the page's only shadow and a rounded panel inside it;
   - a middle-dot eyebrow over the H1 and centred 102ch paragraphs, plus the same stock microscope and scientist on every study page.
3. **Convergence cluster:** 4, the SaaS card kit, with cluster-5 chrome (the middle-dot eyebrow).
4. **Disposition:**
   - **Raikou: FIX (5.60)**, for P2, P3, P5 and the template items.
   - **Badenhorst: FIX (5.49)**, for P1 (the blocker), P4 and the template items.
   - Design is above 5.0 on both, so this is not an automatic REBUILD. But there is no direction contract and no visual signature for the study-page type, so fix cycles alone will probably level off around 6.5. Going higher is an art-direction question, not a CSS one.
   - **Fix the template items (T1–T5) before Wang 2013, Ye 2026 or Robinson 2005 are rebuilt on it.** Each new page copies every one of them.

**Reference diff:** no `02-reference-rules.md` and no reference captures exist, so no external comparison was possible. Judged against the house art direction ("Clinical Luminism"):

- Badenhorst desktop follows it: text on the quiet left, subject in the right third, 17:1 contrast.
- Raikou desktop and Badenhorst phone break it: the loud part of the image sits under the type.

## The single change that would most improve both pages

(The two contrast faults, P1 and P2, must be fixed whatever else is done.)

**Change:** turn the answer section into a real reading column. Left-align it at about 65 characters per line, and set the answer paragraph clearly above body size.

**Why:** it is the first thing the visitor reads, it holds the paragraph AI engines quote, and it fixes two measured failures at once (102ch centred prose and a 15px answer).

**Cost:** one stock `rich-text` section's `custom_css`, under 500 characters with no `content:`; about 20–30 minutes; no effect on translations; it applies to every study page at once.
