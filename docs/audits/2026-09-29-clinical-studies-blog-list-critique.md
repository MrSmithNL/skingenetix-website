## Answers

1. **Signature: none.** The candidate is in the copy: each card asks a question and answers with a number against its control
   ("fell 7.4% in 20 days ... while it rose 4.3% on placebo"). But the design sets that number in 14-15px body under a 36px
   question, so the finding is the quietest thing on the card.
2. **Three tells that this was assembled rather than designed:**
   - two badge systems on four cards: a pill above the lead title, and 9-11px pills pinned to photo corners;
   - the theme's "Search for..." in 32px bold, the loudest words in the first screen, naming no scope;
   - a grading legend with nothing graded on the page, opening "How we grade." under "How we grade the evidence". CMS strings
     ("All posts", "Show products matching tag") leak through too.
3. **Convergence cluster: 4 (the card kit), lightly.** Identical 10px-rounded image cards in a symmetric three-up row with pill
   badges; no shadows or gradient washes. It comes from the stock Impact blog list, which the brief requires, so it follows the
   brief rather than defaulting. The cost shows in Creativity.
4. **Weighted total 5.71. Disposition: FIX.** Design is above 5.0, so this is not a REBUILD, and most faults are section-CSS sized.
   - Before the page replaces the live list: F1 (label contrast), F3 (search focus and size), F5 (badge size) and F4 (locales, if
     not already gated).
   - Then F2, which decides whether ask 3 is met, and F6.
   - With no contract and no named signature, and with stock sections as a firm rule, fix cycles will probably level off
     around 6.5-7.

## Reference diff

- **Hairgenetix `/blogs/articles` (Malcolm's reference).** Ours is stronger on title legibility (their white sans title runs over
  a bright product box), on hierarchy (one lead study instead of a flat three-up grid) and on search, which they lack. Ours is
  weaker on the label row, where they set every label in full ink and underline the active one while ours fades the rest to
  3.24:1. It is also weaker on per-card labels, which they set as readable chips under the text while ours pins one 9-11px pill to
  the photo. (Their chips are plain text, not links, like our badges.)
- **The live list.** The preview fixes the grey band: the title is on the photo, and the page is 190px shorter at 1440 and 216px
  shorter at 390. On the phone the live page shows the photo clean with the title on bone; the preview trades that for centred
  type across the glassware (F11).
- No `02-reference-rules.md` exists, so there are no "ten rules for this site" to check.

## The single change that would most improve the page

**Make the label row and the card labels one legible system.** That means full-ink labels with a non-colour mark on the active
one, at least 44px tall on the phone, with a visible focus ring. The card badges should be the same size as the labels on every
card (the lead card's treatment, not the 9px corner pill). This clears F1, F5 and F13 and delivers ask 2 as Malcolm pictured it.

**Cost:** two CSS blocks, one inside the Custom Liquid (no cap) and one on main-blog (about 300 characters free). No new section;
about an hour including the re-measure at 390. F2 (search that finds authors and INCI names) is the must-fix for ask 3, but it
is content work, not a CSS change.
