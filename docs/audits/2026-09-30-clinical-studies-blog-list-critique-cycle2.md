## Answers

1. **Signature: none yet.** There are two candidates, and the page carries neither. The first is in the copy: each card answers its
   question with a number against its control, but that number is set in 17px body under a 36px question. The second is the
   ingredient-coloured science image: the lead's grey skin surface is memorable, and the three images beside it do not follow
   its key.
2. **Three tells that this was assembled rather than designed:**
   - two badge treatments for one label, one of them a pill pinned to a photo corner;
   - a card row whose titles come in two formats, so the row steps by 87px and 113px;
   - a GHK-Cu frame of flask, beaker, dish and microscope that repeats the hero's laboratory-stock vocabulary, next to three
     frames that each use a different key.
3. **Convergence cluster: 4 (the card kit), lightly, and less than in cycle 1.** The cards are still the stock Impact list with
   10px rounded images and pill badges, but there are no shadows and the images now vary. It follows the brief (stock sections
   first).
4. **Weighted total 6.55 (cycle 1: 5.71). Disposition: FIX.** This is an improving revision: keep cycle 2. One cycle remains
   under the three-cycle cap.
   - The fixes for the next cycle are F11 (first screen), N1 (image set), N2 (concern row) and F9 (title formats).
   - With stock sections as a firm rule and no contract, a third cycle can reasonably reach about 7.2-7.5. Beyond that, the
     ceiling is the missing signature, not the fixes.

## The single change that would most improve the page

**Bring the lead study into the first screen at 390 and at 1440**, meaning its image top, title and finding. That takes a shorter
hero band on the phone and a label block that costs one line on the phone instead of four. Whether that is a scrolling row or a
collapsed concern row is the builder's call.

**Cost:** hero section settings plus the labels section's own `<style>` (no cap). No new section. About an hour including the
re-measure at 390 and 200%. N1 (the image set) is the other large gain, but it is asset work, not layout.
