## Answers

1. **Signature.** There is no visual signature. The content idea is distinctive: every study behind every ingredient on one page,
   graded, with a plain-English "What it found" that gives numbers, comparators and who paid. But the design makes the grade the
   smallest and faintest element in each row, and its colour changes meaning from table to table (F2, F3).
2. **Three tells that this was assembled rather than designed:**
   - "→" on every one of 38 links, under ALL-CAPS tracked column heads;
   - the same sentence five times ("N studies, graded. What it is and what the evidence shows is explained on the X research page.")
     under five identical "X studies" headings;
   - grade colours applied mechanically, so A changes colour five times while B wears one ingredient's gold throughout; plus two
     heading systems stitched together (F5).
3. **Convergence cluster:** 5 (template chrome), lightly. The cluster-4 card kit that marked the study pages is gone.
4. **Disposition: FIX (5.75).**
   - Before the page is linked anywhere: F1 (phone hero contrast), F2 (ground), F12 (German copy).
   - Then F3–F5, which decide whether it reads as one designed page.
   - Design is above 5.0, so this is not a REBUILD. But with no direction contract and no named signature for the research section,
     fix cycles alone will probably level off around 6.5.

**Reference diff:** no `02-reference-rules.md` exists. The decision record names SkinCeuticals Clinical Studies, Augustinus Bader
`/evidence/` and Examine.com, but no captures of them exist, so no side-by-side was possible. Against the house art direction: the
desktop banner honours the reserved type zone and the phone banner breaks it.

## The single change that would most improve the page

(F1, the phone hero contrast, must be fixed whatever else is done.)

**Change:** put the five evidence sections on white, as they are on the hubs, and turn the closing section to bone, so the grounds
alternate bone, white, bone.

**Why:**

- It restores the component the decision promised.
- It removes three measured AA failures: meta 4.35 → 4.96, glutathione 4.48 → 5.11, the invisible Review pill.
- It makes the index a distinct band between the intro and the close (F8).

**Cost:** a background setting or a one-line section Custom CSS on six sections, well under 500 characters each. The custom-html
bodies are not touched, so no translation is invalidated. About 20 minutes.
