**For:** the article plan `docs/article-plan-2026-10-09.md`. **Asked by Malcolm, 2026-10-09:** find the other keywords we
should write for, to rank first in Google and in AI answers; plan the articles; tear down the pages holding the top 3
positions for each planned keyword; make sure no new article competes with existing content.

**Spend:** DataForSEO about USD 9.60 (keyword pull 6.68, clickstream enrichment 2.62, filter tests 0.04, 36 desktop SERPs
0.14, 18 mobile SERPs 0.07, authority under 0.05). Search Console free. Account balance before: about USD 435.

## Why a new pull was needed

The 2026-09-22 strategy pull (`scripts/keyword-strategy.py`) used DataForSEO's phrase-match suggestions on ingredient
names. A keyword had to contain "pdrn", "copper peptide", "argireline" and so on to be collected, and each seed stopped at
its top 700 by Ads volume. So no topic that does not name one of our ingredients was ever measured: crow's feet, forehead
lines, smile lines, at-home microneedling, the "salmon sperm facial", polynucleotides, "copper uglies". The 15 planned
spokes in `docs/content-plan-2026.md` §5 were sized on Ads buckets only and never checked against observed searches.

## Key measurements

- **Demand by cluster** (observed searches a month, sum of unclaimed topical terms, US / GB): at-home microneedling and
  derma stamps 50,919 / 17,313 (most of it the shop term "derma stamp"); smile lines 20,505 / 6,725; polynucleotides,
  salmon and skin boosters 18,703 / 20,321 (GB "polynucleotides" alone 4,750, clinic intent); crow's feet and eye lines
  15,234 / 3,719; copper 11,383 / 2,291 ("copper uglies" 1,157 US); forehead 6,427 / 2,213. The question long tail of our
  own ingredients is small: 174 PDRN terms sum to 2,354 US, 134 Argireline terms to 1,201.
- **AI Overviews on 35 of 36 SERPs; they cite us on none.** Most-cited: YouTube 27, Instagram 16, Google 11, Cleveland
  Clinic 9, Healthline 8, Reddit 7, Facebook 6.
- **The top 3 rarely cite research.** Of the measured top-3 pages, almost all link to no study at all (BBC's "salmon
  sperm" piece, 20 links, is the exception). Our study articles cite 6 to 12 sources each.
- **Page-level authority of #1 is low** (0 to 22 referring domains on the URL, except Cleveland Clinic and Healthline at
  87 to 258), **domain-level authority is not** (small clinics 40 to 1,500 referring domains; ours 2, of which 0 genuine).
