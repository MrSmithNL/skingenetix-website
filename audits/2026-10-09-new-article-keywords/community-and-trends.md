# Community questions and 2026 trends: demand that keyword tools do not see

**Date:** 2026-10-09. **Method:** web research by a research subagent (read-only), cross-checked here against the
keyword pull in this folder. **Limits:** live Reddit and Quora threads did not load through the search tool, so the
recurrence of each question is inferred from editorial coverage, brand FAQs and trend data, not counted from Reddit.
Several trend sources are retailer or brand blogs; treat their numbers as directional. Nothing here is used as a claim on
the site; it only steers which questions to measure.

## Candidate questions the research surfaced (before measurement)

Tier A (strongest demand evidence and a gap): does topical PDRN penetrate skin; PDRN serum vs PDRN cream; topical PDRN vs a
"salmon sperm facial"; PDRN with vitamin C, niacinamide and acids; copper peptide purging and "copper uglies"; how long
peptide serums take to work; do peptides in skincare work; what to put on skin after a derma stamp; derma stamp vs derma
roller; how often to use a derma stamp.

Tier B: copper peptides with microneedling; copper peptides with niacinamide; copper peptides with AHAs and BHAs; peptide
serum layering order; peptides as a retinol alternative; peptides in pregnancy; PDRN and fish allergy; PDRN for mature skin;
a PDRN and copper routine; how long Argireline takes; Argireline with retinol or vitamin C; Matrixyl serum vs cream;
reading peptide names on a label.

Tier C (trend-led, lower fit or higher risk): PDRN vs exosomes; at-home "skin boosters"; peptide stacking; how to patch
test; purging vs reacting; how much PDRN is in a serum (1% = 10,000 ppm); does topical glutathione work; PDRN vs hyaluronic
acid; skin longevity.

Excluded at source: injectables as the subject, oral supplements, hair, whitening, medical conditions (fungal acne,
eczema, rosacea), "best peptide serum", and formats we do not sell (eye patches, masks, toners).

Every candidate above was then measured: observed search volume in `candidates-US.json` and `candidates-GB.json`, and the
live SERP and top-3 teardown in `serp/` and `teardowns/`. The plan uses only those measurements.
