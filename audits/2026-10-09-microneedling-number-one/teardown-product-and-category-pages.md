## 0. The findings that matter most

1. **We are absent from every commercial surface, not just from organic results.** Our pages rank on none of the 38 SERPs [SERP], appear in
   none of the 31 Google popular-products blocks, which sit on 19 of the 38 SERPs (Dr Pen holds 37 distinct product slots on 14 of those 19,
   in all four markets, plus 13 more on 4 SERPs of the earlier pull) [SERP] [SERP-0],
   and no AI answer cites skingenetix.com, while the sister site hairgenetix.com is cited 4 times on the PDRN / copper prompt [AI]. VERIFIED.
2. **Our product pages are 108 to 212 words with no H2, no how-to, no frequency, no cleaning or safety section, no FAQ, no video and no
   reviews** [FETCH] [PG]. The #1 stamp page (Dr Pen US) has 885 measured words, a sterilising method, a needle-by-needle frequency table, a
   replacement schedule and star ratings in schema [PG] [FETCH]. VERIFIED.
3. **The format the AI answers recommend is not the format we sell.** Perplexity and Claude recommend a single-use, sterile, shallow (0.25
   to 0.5 mm) stamp head with a sealed single-dose serum, used every 2 weeks [AI]. We sell one reusable 0.5 mm stamp with no stated cleaning or
   replacement schedule, four single-use vials and a "one-month ritual" that implies weekly use [FETCH]. EvenSkyn sells almost exactly the
   format the answers describe (four gamma-sterilised single-use 0.5 mm heads, four sealed PDRN ampoules, every 2 weeks, "2-month supply",
   €109.95) and is cited 13 times [FETCH] [AI]. VERIFIED.
4. **Dr Pen wins on exact-match names, catalogue breadth, stars and brand demand, not on links.** Its winning product and collection pages
   carry 0 to 4 page-level referring domains on 42 to 108-domain storefronts [BL]; "dr pen" is searched 12,835 times a month in the US [VOL]. It
   also makes device-type claims (scars, deep wrinkles, "80-85% increase in collagen production", 1.5 mm for stretch marks at home) that our
   regulation research forbids us [FETCH] [REG]. VERIFIED. We cannot copy its claims; we can copy its structure.
5. **Our live copy carries three phrases the regulation research puts on the "cannot say" list:** "absorb deeper" and "absorb more
   effectively" (both stamp pages), "fine sterile stamp" (both meta descriptions, while the page calls the stamp "reusable"), and "deliver our
   peptide serums through fine, sterile micro-channels" (collection) [FETCH] [REG]. Both sets are "Pre-order" with `InStock` schema and no
   ship date [FETCH]. VERIFIED.
6. **The bare term "derma stamp" belongs to Hairgenetix's plan** (its stamp set is the declared hero for "derma stamp", 18,100 Ads bucket)
   [hairgenetix `docs/microneedling-number-one-plan-2026-10-09.md`]. Skingenetix should own the face variants ("microneedling stamp",
   "derma stamp for face", "home microneedling kit"), not split the bare term across two of Malcolm's domains. VERIFIED (the plan) and PLAUSIBLE
   (the cannibalisation risk).

## 2. Dr Pen teardown

### 2.1 How the site is built [FETCH], VERIFIED

- **One brand, many storefronts, all cross-declared.** Every product page carries hreflang to drpen.com.au, drpen.co.nz, us.drpen.co,
  uk.drpen.co (own handles, e.g. `/products/derma-stamp-micro-needling-skin-tool`), and eu.drpen.co with language folders (`/de/`, `/it/`,
  `/fr/`, `/nl/`, `/ru/`, `/fi/`, `/sv/`, `/hu/`, `/sk/`). The AU blog ranks in the US (#1 "best microneedling serum", #2 "how to use a derma
  stamp") [SERP-0]. Independent distributors add more domains: dr-pen.co.uk (115 domains; #1 GB "at home microneedling", #2 GB "home
  microneedling kit") and drpengermany.com (1,018 domains; cited in the DE "microneedling wie oft" Overview) [SERP] [BL].
- **Category tree:** about 38 collections on the US store, including one per pen model, cartridges, nanoneedling cartridges, ampoules,
  "post-treatment", "with-treatment", derma rollers, bundles and kits.
- **Category to product:** strong. The Derma Rollers collection lists 14 SKUs (rollers at 128, 250, 540 and 1,200 pins in 0.25 and 0.5 mm,
  three stamps, two hydra stamps in 0.25 to 1.5 mm, the micro-infusion stamp kit, two roller kits); the UK serums collection lists 31 serums
  and ampoules with filters "For Microneedling (19)", "Post-Microneedling (3)", "In-Between Microneedling (7)".
- **Guide to product:** present. "The Best Ampoules to Use with Microneedling" links 4 products (Medisco HA, Medisco MesO PDRN, Medisco
  Glutathione, Stayve kit); the DE "Was ist Derma Stamping?" links 4 (derma stamp, H24 hydra stamp, roller kit).
- **Product to guide:** absent. Product pages link only to upsells (Peachaboo HA serum, Bio Needle stamp) and the 2-minute pen quiz.
- **Template on every product page:** an unsourced stat block "90% visible improvements in fine lines and wrinkles ... 80-85% increase in
  collagen production", "1-year warranty, lifetime support", free shipping banner, "Talk to an expert", and a disclaimer "This product may not
  have been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration".

### 2.3 Why a 42 to 108-domain site wins (VERIFIED facts, PLAUSIBLE weighting)

1. **Names equal queries.** `/products/derma-stamp` titled "Derma Stamp"; "Derma Rollers"; "All Microneedling Pens"; "Microneedling Serums |
   Serums for Microneedling". Our page names put "Facial" or "PDRN" between "microneedling" and "stamp".
2. **Breadth answers "show me the options".** 14 roller and stamp SKUs, 7 to 10 pens, 31 serums, cartridges per model. Product SERPs are
   choice SERPs: the other top results are Amazon and Target search pages [SERP].
3. **Stars in the result.** Yotpo ratings are written into Product JSON-LD (4.8 from 90 US, 4.7 from 20 EU, 5.0 from 15 on the G10 kit) and
   show in the SERP [FETCH] [SERP].
4. **Merchant listings.** 37 distinct Dr Pen product slots on 14 SERPs in all four markets [SERP]. The block sits at absolute rank 1 to 3
   on six of our target SERPs: GB "derma roller", GB "microneedling serum", GB "microneedling stamp", GB "sterile hyaluronic acid for
   microneedling", DE "dermaroller für gesicht" and DE "microneedling zuhause" [SERP].
5. **Brand demand.** "dr pen" 12,835 US a month, "dr pen microneedling" 2,667 US / 791 GB, "dr pen microneedling cartridges" 201 / 158
   [VOL]. Amazon and marketplace resellers repeat the name.
6. **The page-level bar is low.** Winning Dr Pen pages carry 0 to 4 page-level referring domains [BL].
7. **Not a factor:** content quality or claim discipline. The stamp page recommends 1.5 mm at home for stretch marks and an unsourced
   collagen percentage [FETCH]; the serums collection that ranks #1 is the vitamin-C tag, a serum its own blog says not to needle with [FETCH].
